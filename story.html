Our Story

Picture this: It's Sunday night. You've saved 47 healthy recipe reels this week. Made exactly zero. The quinoa is still in the back of your cabinet, judging you. Sound familiar?

We started The Honest Cuisine because we were tired of the gap between "I want to eat healthy" and actually doing it. So we thought — what if someone made all those beautiful Instagram recipes for you? Fresh, every weekend, delivered to your door?

Then we looked at Mumbai's "healthy" food scene. Overpriced grain bowls that cost more than your Uber. The same sad menu for 6 months straight. Restaurants that slap "organic" on the label and charge you double. We were not impressed.

So we built the kitchen we wished existed. New menu every single weekend. Seasonal produce from actual farms. Experimental dishes that keep you coming back because you never know what's next. And prices that don't require a salary hike.

No maida. No refined sugar. No ingredients your body can't pronounce. Just real food, made by real people, with nothing to hide.

Also our brownies are insanely good and we will not apologise for bragging about that. 🍫

# The Honest Cuisine — Brand Guidelines

---

## 1. Brand Overview

**Name:** The Honest Cuisine
**Tagline:** "nothing to hide"

**Brand Story**

Everyone scrolls past beautiful healthy food on Instagram. Saves the reels. Never makes any of it. The Honest Cuisine bridges that gap — we make the food you wish you could cook, fresh every weekend, delivered to your door in Mumbai.

Mumbai's "healthy" restaurants are overpriced, boring, and serve the same menu for months. We're the opposite. New dishes every week. Seasonal ingredients. Experimental kitchen. Prices that make sense.

The menu changes every weekend because we cook what's fresh, not what's sitting in a warehouse. No refined flour, no refined sugar, no artificial preservatives. Nothing to hide.

**Core Principles**

- New menu every weekend — seasonal, experimental, never repetitive
- Bridging the gap between recipe reels and real meals
- Radical transparency in ingredients and process
- Affordable healthy food — not a luxury wellness tax
- Weekend ordering model — quality over scale

## 2. Logo Usage

### Primary Logo

The primary logo is the **calligraphic THC monogram** paired with the full brand name.

- **Monogram:** Flowing script letters T, H, C intertwined as one continuous line. A small leaf is integrated into the curve of the H. Five dots arranged in an arc above the monogram.
- **Wordmark:** "THE HONEST CUISINE" spelled out below in clean, evenly spaced capitals.
- **Default colorway:** Warm Charcoal `#3A3632` on Warm Cream `#F7F4EF` background.

Always use the primary logo on websites, packaging, menus, and any material where space allows.

### Sub-mark

The **monogram only** (without the spelled-out name) is used for:

- Social media profile pictures
- Favicon / app icon
- Sticker seals and stamps
- Watermarks

The sub-mark may only be used when the full brand name appears elsewhere on the same material (e.g., in a bio, on the same page, on the packaging).

### Clear Space

Maintain clear space around the logo equal to the height of the letter "H" in the monogram on all sides. No text, graphics, or edges should enter this zone.

### Minimum Size

- **Primary logo:** No smaller than 30mm wide (print) / 120px wide (screen).
- **Sub-mark:** No smaller than 12mm wide (print) / 48px wide (screen).

### Do's

- Use on solid cream, white, or charcoal backgrounds
- Use the provided files — never recreate from scratch
- Maintain original proportions at all times
- Use the reversed version (cream on charcoal) for dark backgrounds

### Don'ts

- Never display the abbreviation "THC" as text without the full name nearby
- Never distort, stretch, rotate, or skew the logo
- Never add drop shadows, glows, or outlines
- Never place on busy photographs or patterned backgrounds
- Never change the logo colors outside the approved palette
- Never rearrange the monogram and wordmark relationship

## 4. Typography

The brand typography mirrors the logo: clean, light-weight, widely spaced sans-serif for all brand and heading text. A serif is used only for dish names and editorial accents.

### Primary Typeface — Josefin Sans

*Source: Google Fonts (free)*

This is the brand's core typeface — matching the "THE HONEST CUISINE" text in the logo. Used for all headings, brand text, labels, and UI.

| Style | Weight | Use | Size (Desktop) | Size (Mobile) |
|-------|--------|-----|-----------------|----------------|
| Light | 300 | Brand name, section headings (uppercase, letter-spacing 0.4–0.5em) | 16–24px | 14–18px |
| Extra-Light | 200 | Tagline, labels, captions, subtle text | 12–14px | 11–13px |
| Regular | 400 | Navigation, buttons, prices | 14–16px | 12–14px |

### Secondary Typeface — Cormorant Garamond

*Source: Google Fonts (free)*

Used sparingly for editorial warmth — dish names, quotes, and storytelling text.

| Style | Weight | Use | Size (Desktop) | Size (Mobile) |
|-------|--------|-----|-----------------|----------------|
| Italic | Regular (400i) | Dish/item names, pull quotes | 20–28px | 18–22px |
| Light | 300 | Body text, descriptions, long-form | 16–18px | 14–16px |

### Hierarchy Rules

1. **Brand Name / Page Title:** Josefin Sans Light, 18–24px, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.5em, Warm Charcoal
2. **Section Heading:** Josefin Sans Light, 14–18px, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.4em, Warm Charcoal
3. **Dish/Item Name:** Cormorant Garamond Italic, 20–28px, Warm Charcoal
4. **Body Text:** Josefin Sans Light, 14–16px, normal case, Warm Charcoal
5. **Labels & Tagline:** Josefin Sans Extra-Light, 12–14px, italic or uppercase, letter-spacing 0.3em
6. **Buttons & Tags:** Josefin Sans Regular, 14px, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.15em

The logo monogram uses a custom calligraphic script — this is NOT replicated in any other brand material. The monogram exists only in the logo file.

Never use more than these two typefaces in any single piece of content.

## 5. Photography Style

### Principles

- **Natural light only.** No flash. No studio strobes. Soft, diffused daylight preferred.
- **Overhead and 45-degree angles.** Flat lays for full spreads, angled for texture and depth.
- **Surfaces:** Cream linen, light wood, raw stone, terracotta. Avoid glossy or metallic surfaces.
- **Fresh ingredients visible.** Show the raw alongside the cooked — herbs, whole spices, sliced vegetables.
- **No artificial styling.** No inedible garnishes. No glycerin spray. Food should look touchable, not shellacked.
- **Breathing room.** Leave negative space. Not every inch needs to be filled. Let the cream background show.

### Color Temperature

Warm and slightly desaturated. Avoid oversaturated greens or artificially orange-tinted food. Edit to match the brand palette — creams, sage, earthy tones.

### What to Avoid

- Neon or artificial-looking food
- Dark, moody food photography
- Cluttered or props-heavy compositions
- Stock photography of any kind
- Hands holding food (unless clearly Juhi's / the chef's — real, not staged)

## 6. Social Media Guidelines

### Instagram

- **Handle:** @thehonestcuisine
- **Profile Picture:** THC monogram centered on a solid cream circle (`#F7F4EF`). Alternate: monogram on charcoal circle (`#3A3632`) with cream logo.
- **Bio tone:** Short. Honest. No buzzwords. Example: *"Weekend meals. Real ingredients. Nothing to hide. Mumbai."*

### Story Highlight Covers

Solid pastel circles with a thin Josefin Sans label in charcoal:

| Highlight | Circle Color |
|-----------|-------------|

| Process | Butter `#F0E6C8` |
| Recipes | Sage `#C5D1B6` |
| Order | Soft Rose `#F2DEDE` |

### Post Design

- **Background:** Warm Cream `#F7F4EF`
- **Text color:** Warm Charcoal `#3A3632`
- **Fonts in Canva:** Cormorant Garamond (headlines) + Josefin Sans (captions/labels)
- **Layout:** Generous margins. Left-aligned or centered text. Never edge-to-edge.
- **Photo posts:** Warm-toned, natural light photography. No heavy filters.

### Content Approach

- Show the process — sourcing, prepping, cooking, plating
- Name the farms, name the ingredients, name the method
- Recipe content earns trust; selling comes second
- Use the tagline "nothing to hide" as a recurring motif, not a hard sell

## 7. Packaging

### Materials

- **Box:** Kraft paper / cardboard (unbleached, natural brown or cream)
- **Sticker:** Round logo sticker (primary logo) on matte finish, placed to seal the box
- **Insert:** Thank you card on cream stock — handwritten-style message, Cormorant Garamond italic, includes Instagram handle and a personal note
- **Band:** Packaging band in Sage Green `#C5D1B6` wrapping the box, with "nothing to hide" printed in Josefin Sans Light, charcoal text

### Packaging Principles

- No plastic where avoidable
- Minimal ink — let the kraft paper do the talking
- Every touchpoint should feel personal, not mass-produced
- Include ingredient list on a small tag or sticker (radical transparency extends to packaging)

## 8. Voice & Tone

### Personality

Honest. Warm. Approachable. Like a friend who loves feeding people.

"Come eat at my place" energy — not "buy our premium artisanal offering."

### Writing Rules

- Short sentences. Say it simply.
- No jargon. No "curated." No "artisanal." No "elevate your palate."
- Talk about food the way you'd talk about it at your dining table.
- Be specific — "Alibaug sweet potatoes" not "farm-fresh produce."
- Admit what you don't know. Admit when something didn't work out.
- First person when it's personal. Third person when it's the brand speaking officially.

### Examples

**Yes:**
> "This week's dal is made with toor from a farm in Kolhapur. It cooks slower. Tastes better. That's it."

> "We tried a new dessert. It flopped. Back to the classics this weekend."

**No:**
> "Our chef has curated an exquisite menu featuring artisanal ingredients sourced from premium organic farms."

> "Elevate your weekend dining experience with our farm-to-table offerings."

### Tagline Usage

"nothing to hide" — always lowercase. Used as a sign-off, a packaging detail, a quiet statement. Never shouted. Never in all-caps. Never with an exclamation mark.
